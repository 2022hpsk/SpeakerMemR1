## Sources and scope

The complete original benchmark conversation and question payloads are not included. Included training inputs and final result records contain benchmark-derived material and remain subject to the relevant upstream terms. See [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md) for release identifiers, included artifacts, and reproduction instructions.

- **SocialMemBench:** [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/anon4data/socialmembench) and [benchmark code](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/SocialMemBench). Training inputs, trajectories, held-out inputs, and evaluation records are project-derived artifacts, not an unmodified benchmark release. The notice supplied with the local checkout is retained below.
- **GroupMemBench:** [UCSB-NLP-Chang/GroupMemBench](https://github.com/UCSB-NLP-Chang/GroupMemBench). Included evaluation helpers and prompts originate from this benchmark; refer to the upstream terms rather than treating them as newly licensed project code.
- **EverMemBench-Dynamic:** [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/EverMind-AI/EverMemBench-Dynamic) and [code](https://github.com/EverMind-AI/EverMemBench).
